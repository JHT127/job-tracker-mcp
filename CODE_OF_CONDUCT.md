# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, explain disagreements clearly, and respect project boundaries and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted contact, doxxing, and disruptive or abusive conduct are not welcome.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
